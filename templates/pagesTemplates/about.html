About Me

I'm Edwin, a Business Systems Analyst with a background in business integrations and service in the U.S. Navy Reserve. I enjoy figuring out how things work and finding practical ways to solve problems.

In my daily work, I troubleshoot EDI transactions and help businesses exchange information. Learning software development is helping me understand how to build the applications behind those processes.
